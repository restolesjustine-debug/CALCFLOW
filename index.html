THE BIG IDEA

Can we reverse the change?

A derivative tells us how a function changes. An antiderivative helps us rebuild the function that came before it.

✦

Imagine seeing tire tracks and working backward to trace the car’s path. We know the change; now we recover the original.

Three gentle moves.
